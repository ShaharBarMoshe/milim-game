Explorer's Guide

Player Movement & Controls

• Move player using WASD or Arrow Keys.

• Alternatively, use the touch D-Pad at the bottom left or **click/tap directly anywhere on the map** to walk to that spot.

Map Navigation & View

• **Drag / Pan**: Click and drag with mouse or touch gesture to pan camera.

• **Zoom**: Scroll wheel or use the / zoom buttons.

• **Points of Interest**: Click landmark icons directly on map to open lore details.
